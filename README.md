## 🇨🇳 中文

把纯文字旁白或故事脚本做成竖版或横版羊毛毡故事短片：人物、场景、道具全部重新表现为现实世界比例的完整羊毛毡材质，不是微缩模型、立体微缩景观或玩偶舞台。流程是严格节点门控——先锁画幅（默认 9:16，另支持 16:9、1:1、4:3、3:4、21:9）、图片清晰度（1K 或 2K）与视频清晰度（768P 或 2K），确认故事板，再选旁白、对白或混合音频模式，逐镜生成并最终合成。音频时间轴是镜头时长的权威依据：每段旁白与对应镜头等长，完整旁白与对白不得被截断、遗漏或强行加速，混音按已确认时间码放置音轨。角色有变身、伪装、换装等持续状态变化时，每个需要出现的状态都单独定义并生成专属参考图，绑定到对应镜头。不适用于静态图片或微缩类项目。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 文字旁白/脚本 → 竖版或横版的羊毛毡成片
- 严格门控：画幅、图片清晰度、视频清晰度逐项锁定
- 音频时间轴定镜头时长：逐镜记录旁白起止、对白起止、空隙与总长
- 三种音频模式：旁白、角色对白、混合，旁白与对白不重叠
- 状态变体：变身/伪装/换装角色各自专属参考图并绑定对应镜头
- 成片与 BGM 版分开交付，始终保留无 BGM 的旁白版

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns plain narration or a story script into a vertical or horizontal wool-felt short in which people, sets and props are all re-imagined as a full wool-felt world at real-world proportions — not a miniature, diorama or doll stage. The process is strictly gate-controlled: lock the aspect ratio (9:16 by default, plus 16:9, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4 and 21:9), image clarity (1K or 2K) and video clarity (768P or 2K), confirm the storyboard, choose narration, dialogue or hybrid audio mode, then generate shot by shot and composite. The audio timeline is the authority on shot duration: every narration line matches its shot's length, and full narration or dialogue may never be truncated, dropped or sped up, with tracks placed at the confirmed timecodes. When a character changes state through transformation, disguise or wardrobe, each state gets its own reference image bound to the right shots. Not for static images or miniature-style projects.

### ✨ What it does

- Plain narration or script into a finished vertical or horizontal felt short
- Strict gates: aspect ratio, image clarity and video clarity each locked in turn
- Audio timeline sets shot duration, with per-shot narration start/end, dialogue start/end, gap and total
- Three audio modes: narration, character dialogue, hybrid, with narration and dialogue never overlapping
- State variants: transformation, disguise or wardrobe changes each get a dedicated reference image bound to their shots
- Final cut and BGM version shipped separately, always keeping the narration-only cut without BGM
